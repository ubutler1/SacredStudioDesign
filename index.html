02 / THE PRACTICE

A practice rooted in observation.

Alexander approaches the figure with close attention to likeness, proportion and light. His paintings extend beyond representation through shifts in color, texture and geometric form. Portraiture and urban subjects offer distinct starting points for this ongoing exploration.
