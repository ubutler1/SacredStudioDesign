# Uriel Alexander Art Studio: logo use

Approved by the artist in Website HQ & Launch, October 30, 2026. This identity update applies to the review branch; public release remains a separate approval.

The primary identity combines the supplied gold palette-and-brush symbol with the black Uriel Alexander wordmark and ART STUDIO line. Use the supplied artwork as a complete lockup. Keep its proportions and original colors.

## Placement and consistency

- Use the complete transparent logo in the header on all seven pages. The logo links home and has an accessible studio-name label.
- Use the gold symbol with the existing readable artist name in the dark footer. Keep the full black wordmark on light backgrounds.
- Leave clear space around the logo. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, redraw or add effects to the supplied artwork.
- Preserve the existing warm neutral, burgundy and restrained gold website design, approved headline and nine-work gallery presentation.
- Use Uriel Alexander for the professional artist name, Uriel Alexander Art Studio for the studio, and @urielalexanderstudio for Instagram.

## Studio applications

The supplied card, box, mug and shirt mockups illustrate possible uses on correspondence, packaging and studio goods. They are appearance references, not active products or production specifications. Use the same master identity for future business cards, packaging inserts, email-signature artwork and social branding. Keep essential contact details as readable text alongside the logo.

Before producing physical materials, check the final size, background, color and legibility with the chosen production process. The supplied PNG masters and the website SVG wrapper are not original vector outlines. Retain these masters unchanged when preparing any later production files.

No merchandise, print/download listing, price, checkout, social-account change or email-signature change is activated by this website update.
