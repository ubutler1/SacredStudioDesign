Meet Uriel Alexander, a visual artist working through color, figures, and the city from a studio practice in the Bronx, New York.

THE STORY IN THE WORK

Every surface has something underneath.

Uriel Alexander is a Bronx-based visual artist creating expressive portraits and city-inspired paintings. Distinctive faces, exaggerated gestures, familiar neighborhood architecture, and unexpected combinations of color sit side by side.

The paintings build in layers: transparent passages meet opaque paint, architectural lines pass through a figure, and traces of earlier decisions remain visible. That tension between structure and expressive marks is part of what gives the work its presence.

Across the collection, people and their surroundings share the frame. A city scene becomes intimate, and a portrait opens into a world of its own.
