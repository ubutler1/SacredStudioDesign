Uriel Alexander Art Studio

I am Uriel Alexander, a Bronx-based visual artist exploring how identity changes through shifts in place, memory, and personal experience. My paintings bring the figure together with architectural, botanical, and geometric structures to consider what we carry forward when our surroundings and circumstances change.

I use layered color, transparent and opaque passages, and selective realism to let earlier decisions remain visible beneath newer ones. This process reflects the idea that becoming someone new does not require erasing who we have been.

The studio is building a collection of original paintings and plans to offer carefully reproduced fine-art prints and personal-use digital editions.
