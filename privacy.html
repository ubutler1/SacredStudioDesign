STUDIO / PRIVACY

Privacy and your choices.

How information is used when you browse the collection or contact the studio.

About this notice

Uriel Alexander Art Studio, based in the Bronx, New York, operates this portfolio website. This notice covers browsing the website, the inquiry form and correspondence with the studio. For privacy questions or requests, email urielalexanderstudio@gmail.com.

Studio inquiries

The contact form asks for your name, email address, inquiry topic and message. The studio uses these details to respond, discuss the work or project you describe and keep the related correspondence. Submitting an inquiry does not subscribe you to a mailing list.

Netlify processes and stores form submissions. Notifications and subsequent correspondence are handled through the studio's Google Gmail account. Inquiry information may therefore be held in both services. The form has no file-upload or payment fields. Please keep card details, passwords, identity documents and sensitive personal information out of your message.

The studio does not send your inquiry content or email address to Pinterest through this website's integration.

Hosting and website analytics

Netlify hosts the website and processes technical request information, which can include IP addresses, requested pages, referring pages, browser or device details and request times. Netlify Web Analytics provides the studio with reports such as pageviews, traffic sources and approximate visitor locations. It uses server logs rather than analytics cookies.

These hosting and analytics requests are separate from optional Pinterest measurement. Choosing Decline below keeps the Pinterest tag off; it does not stop the technical requests needed to serve the website or Netlify's server-side analytics.

Optional Pinterest measurement

If you choose to allow it, the Pinterest tag helps measure visits to the studio's public portfolio pages. Pinterest may receive information such as the page address, referring page, browser and device information, IP address and cookie identifiers. This information is shared with Pinterest for measurement and personalized advertising.

The tag stays off until you allow it. It is not loaded on Contact, the inquiry confirmation or this Privacy page, or when the page address contains a query string or fragment. The studio's integration does not pass contact-form fields or email identifiers to Pinterest.

Your choice is stored locally in this browser until you change it or clear the browser's stored data. You can change it below at any time. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track keep Pinterest measurement off. Turning measurement off stops future collection through this integration; it does not erase information already sent to Pinterest or remove cookies set by Pinterest. Use Pinterest's settings or your browser's cookie controls for those additional choices.

Your choice

Pinterest privacy options

You can also manage personalized advertising through Pinterest's privacy and data settings or AdChoices. Read Pinterest's privacy policy for information about its use of data.

Optional studio updates

Studio updates require a separate, unchecked opt-in. The signup form asks for your email address and records the consent statement with your request. An ordinary artwork or commission inquiry does not subscribe you.

The studio manages this list directly using its private inquiry records and email account. Your address is used for occasional emails about new paintings, future print releases and exhibitions. No separate mailing platform is connected by this signup. To unsubscribe or ask for deletion, reply to a studio email or contact urielalexanderstudio@gmail.com. The studio will remove you from future studio-update mailings; provider recovery copies may remain under their retention policies.

Service providers and external links

Netlify and Google process information to provide hosting, forms and email services. Depending on the service, information may be processed in the United States or other countries. Their handling of data, including their own retention practices, is described in Netlify's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest links take you to separate services with their own privacy policies. The studio uses ordinary links to those profiles rather than embedded social feeds.

Keeping inquiry records

Inquiry correspondence is kept for as long as needed to respond, carry out related work or address a continuing business or legal need. An inquiry that becomes a commission or sale may require additional records. This website does not currently accept payments or provide customer accounts.

You can ask the studio to delete correspondence that is no longer needed. Deletion from a studio account may not immediately remove copies retained by a service provider under its own policies or legal obligations.

Your questions and requests

To ask about, access, correct or request deletion of the information you have provided, email urielalexanderstudio@gmail.com. If possible, write from the address used for your inquiry and identify the relevant conversation. Please do not send identity documents or other sensitive information with the request.

The studio may need to verify that a request relates to your information. It will explain any records that need to be retained for an ongoing project or legal obligation. Rights and any applicable response deadlines depend on your circumstances and applicable law. Information about another person will not be disclosed in response to your request.

Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated when the website's data practices change. The date below identifies the current version.
