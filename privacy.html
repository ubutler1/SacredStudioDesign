Privacy and your choices.

How the studio handles inquiries and optional Pinterest measurement.

Studio inquiries

The contact form asks for your name, email, inquiry topic and message. Netlify processes the form submission, and the studio uses the information to respond to your inquiry. Please keep payment details and sensitive personal information out of your message.

Optional Pinterest measurement

If you choose to allow it, the Pinterest tag helps measure visits to the studio's public portfolio pages. Pinterest may receive information such as the page address, referring page, browser and device information, IP address and cookie identifiers. This information is shared with Pinterest for measurement and personalized advertising.

The tag stays off until you allow it. It is not loaded on Contact, the inquiry confirmation or this Privacy page. The studio's integration does not pass contact-form fields or email identifiers to Pinterest.

Your choice is stored in this browser. You can change it below at any time. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track keep Pinterest measurement off. Turning measurement off stops future collection through this integration; it does not erase information already sent to Pinterest.

Pinterest privacy options
