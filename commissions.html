The Commission Process

A clean, collaborative workflow—so you feel confident at every stage.

  1. Inquiry — Share your idea, size, deadline, and budget range.
  2. Direction — We align on concept, palette, references, and materials.
  3. Build — Creation begins; you receive progress updates at checkpoints.
  4. Delivery — Final sealing, photography, and pickup/shipping coordination.

What I’ll Need From You

  • Preferred size (ex: 24×36, 36×48)
  • Color direction or room photos (optional)
  • Timeline / deadline
  • Reference images (if portrait)

Commission FAQ

How long do commissions take?

Turnaround depends on size and complexity. Most pieces are scheduled in a short production window once the direction is finalized.

Do you ship?

Yes. Shipping is coordinated after final sealing and packing specs are confirmed.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes—where appropriate. Structure can be discussed during inquiry.
