THE STARTING POINT

Tell me what you have in mind.

01

The idea

Who's it for? What's the story behind it? You don't need a fully formed plan—just somewhere to start.

02

The space

Let me know the size you're thinking about, colors you like, and where you'd picture the painting.

03

The timing

If there's an occasion, deadline, or budget to keep in mind, include it. We'll talk through what's possible.

Every commission starts with a conversation. If it's a good fit, we'll agree on the scope, price, deposit, timing, and delivery before I begin.
