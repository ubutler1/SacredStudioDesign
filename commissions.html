COMMISSIONS / ORIGINAL PAINTINGS

A painting made for you.

A commission offers the opportunity to develop an original work around a subject, setting or personal idea. Each proposal is considered in relation to the artist's practice.

THE COMMISSION PROCESS

Begin with the essentials.

01

The subject

Describe the subject or concept you have in mind. Reference photographs or examples may be useful when appropriate.

02

The scale

Include your preferred dimensions, the intended location of the work and any relevant color considerations.

03

The schedule

Share any important dates and budget considerations. Timing and feasibility are discussed before a commission is accepted.

Commissions are accepted individually. Scope, price, deposit, approval stages, timeline and delivery terms are agreed upon before work begins.
