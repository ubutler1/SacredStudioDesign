COMMISSIONS / ORIGINAL PAINTINGS

A painting made for you.

A commission offers the opportunity to develop an original work around a subject, setting or personal idea. Each proposal is considered in relation to the artist's practice.

Commission inquiries are welcome. The studio is not accepting payments or deposits, or confirming commission start dates, at this time.

Portrait commissions.

Dimensions are width × height. Prices exclude applicable sales tax and delivery. Production estimates include reviews, drying and finishing; any waiting-list period and shipping transit are separate. These are inquiry guidelines, not a booking offer or guaranteed completion date.

Additional figures, full figures, complex architecture, rush requests and commercial use require an individual quote. Scope, total price, review stages, schedule and delivery would be confirmed in writing before a booking.

THE COMMISSION PROCESS

Begin with the essentials.

01

The subject

Describe the subject or concept you have in mind. Reference photographs are welcome when you have permission to share them.

02

The scale

Include your preferred dimensions, the intended location of the work and any relevant color considerations.

03

The schedule

Share your budget range, delivery city or ZIP code, and any important dates. The studio will review the project before confirming feasibility or timing.

Sending an inquiry does not reserve a commission slot. Requests can be discussed while booking arrangements are being completed; no deposit or start-date commitment is being accepted.
