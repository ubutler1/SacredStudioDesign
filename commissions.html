COMMISSIONS / ORIGINAL PAINTINGS

A painting made for you.

A commission offers the opportunity to develop an original work around a subject, setting or personal idea. Each proposal is considered in relation to the artist's practice.

Commission inquiries are welcome. The studio is not accepting payments or deposits, or confirming commission start dates, at this time.

COMMISSIONS BY INQUIRY

Portrait commissions.

Portrait commissions begin at $950 USD.

The starting price covers an 18 × 24 in single-person, head-and-shoulders portrait with a simple background, painted in acrylic and oil on unframed stretched canvas.

Size options, final pricing and estimated timing are shared after reviewing the subject and scope of your inquiry. Each proposal reflects the agreed dimensions, composition and level of detail.

Applicable sales tax and delivery are additional. A written quote would confirm the scope, total price, review stages, schedule and delivery arrangements before any booking.

THE COMMISSION PROCESS

Begin with the essentials.

01

The subject

Describe the subject or concept you have in mind. Reference photographs are welcome when you have permission to share them.

02

The scale

Include your preferred dimensions, the intended location of the work and any relevant color considerations.

03

The schedule

Share your budget range, delivery city or ZIP code, and any important dates. The studio will review the project before confirming feasibility or timing.

Sending an inquiry does not reserve a commission slot. Requests can be discussed while booking arrangements are being completed; no deposit or start-date commitment is being accepted.

BEFORE YOU INQUIRE

Commission questions.

What is included in the starting price?

The $950 USD starting price is for an 18 × 24 in portrait of one person, shown head and shoulders, with a simple background. The painting is acrylic and oil on unframed stretched canvas. Applicable tax and delivery are additional.

Are larger paintings possible?

Portrait sizes also include 24 × 36 in and 30 × 40 in, stated width × height. The studio reviews your reference, composition and preferred size before preparing an individual proposal.

Can a painting be made from a photograph?

Reference photographs are welcome when you have permission to use them and the appropriate consent of the person depicted. Begin with a description in the inquiry form; references can be discussed by email.

How long does a portrait take?

For planning, an 18 × 24 in portrait is estimated at 8–10 weeks to painting-completion review from a confirmed start. Larger works require more time. Drying, finishing and delivery may add time; the written proposal would confirm the project schedule. Start dates are not being confirmed at present.

How do inquiries and booking work?

Send the subject, preferred size, approximate budget, desired timing and delivery city or ZIP code. The studio aims to reply within two business days. Inquiries are welcome, but payments, deposits and confirmed bookings are not open.

How is delivery arranged?

Tell the studio where the painting would need to go. Packing, delivery arrangements and any charges would be confirmed in the written quote before a booking. No fixed shipping charge or delivery date is promised at the inquiry stage.
